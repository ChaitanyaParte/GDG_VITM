About GDG VITM

More than a community.

GDG VITM is where students who like building things find each other. The core team is the group that makes that possible — turning ideas into workshops, hackathons and a community that actually ships.

The core team plans and runs everything: workshops, hackathons, speaker sessions, and the day-to-day of keeping the community active.
